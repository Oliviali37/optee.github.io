Optee Invoice Tracker

An Excel-based invoice & payment tracking tool. Built for small businesses just getting started who want to stay organized without subscriptions.

What it helps with:

  • Create invoices in Excel with automatic invoice numbers
  • Automatically pulls all invoices into a simple summary sheet
  • All files stored locally — no cloud, no accounts
  • No setup, no subscriptions, just work the way you normally do

Just a simple tool to help early-stage businesses stay organized.
